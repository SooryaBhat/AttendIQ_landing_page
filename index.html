How It Works

Two AI modes. One clean result.

01
📸

Face Recognition

Faculty photographs the classroom — even a group photo. The AI (dlib + face_recognition) detects every face, computes 128-dimensional embeddings, and matches against enrolled students. Everyone present is marked automatically.

Voice Biometrics

Students record a voice sample during enrollment. For attendance, faculty records classroom audio. Resemblyzer's GE2E LSTM model creates 256-d speaker embeddings. Cosine similarity identifies each speaker and marks them present.

AI Engine

The intelligence inside

01

Face Recognition

Uses dlib with HOG detection and face_recognition library. Each face → 128-dimensional embedding. SVC classifier matches against enrolled students. Works on single or group classroom photos uploaded by faculty.

dlib HOG128-d embeddingSVC classifierGroup photos
02

Voice Biometrics

Powered by Resemblyzer GE2E LSTM. Student records a voice sample during enrollment. Audio is resampled to 16 kHz mono via librosa and converted to a 256-d speaker embedding. Cosine similarity identifies speakers from classroom recordings.

GE2E LSTM256-d embeddinglibrosa 16kHzcosine similarity
03

Why Two Deployments?

AI libraries (dlib, Resemblyzer, scikit-learn) are too large for Render's free tier. Solution: FastAPI backend on Render handles auth, DB, and routes. A separate Streamlit app on Streamlit Cloud runs the AI models — both share the same Supabase database.
